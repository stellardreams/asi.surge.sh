### 4.1 Summary of Your 3 Foundation Innovations Added to Spec & Issue #103:
1. **Anti-Sink & Anti-Adfreeze Jack-Out Footpads (`120 kN` Breakout Lift)**:

2. **Solar Pop-Can Heater vs. Passive $\text{CO}_2$ Thermo-Siphon**:
   - It is the exact same buoyancy physics! A solar pop-can collector uses sunlight to warm air so it rises into your house (and stops at night when the cans get cold). An Arctic $\text{CO}_2$ thermo-siphon uses `-50 °C` winter wind at the top radiator fins to condense $\text{CO}_2$ vapor and pump heat *out* of the ground all winter—and in summer, when the air is warmer than the permafrost, the warm gas stays at the top and convection **automatically stops**, acting as a one-way thermal check valve!
3. **Formearth Snow + Ice + Gravel Cryo-Composite Ballast (Tier 1) & Machu Picchu Geopolymers (Tier 2 Post-Deadline)**:
   - **Included in DND Proposal (`Tier 1`)**: Filling the `2,157 kg` container ballast bays with local gravel, crushed rock, and **compacted snow/ice super-frozen by a thermo-siphon cooling loop** into a rock-hard cryo-composite block (`> 2,100 kg/m³`, Pykrete principle).
