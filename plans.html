Open Architecture Strategy

Within reason, these master plans are shared as publicly as possible under open principles. These plans are yours to study, adapt, and build upon—provided you link your derived plans back to this project as their source.

To put these concepts into practice: examine each sub-plan critically, analyze the trade-offs of every proposition, decompose single lines into high-level milestones, and test assumptions directly against real-world customer and engineering needs. Build things people genuinely need.
