# Technology Readiness Level (TRL 1–9) Engineering Framework

This guide establishes the standardized Technology Readiness Level (TRL 1–9) definitions, maturity gates, and milestone progression criteria utilized across our aerospace, autonomous manufacturing, and extreme-environment engineering systems.

---

## 📊 Universal TRL Matrix (Definitions & Maturity Gates)

| Level | Title | Core Definition | Engineering Milestone & Exit Gate |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TRL 1** | **Basic Principles Observed & Reported** | Initial scientific research translated into applied engineering formulations. Paper studies of fundamental physics and material properties. | Mathematical formulations documented; initial feasibility assessed. |
| **TRL 2** | **Technology Concept Formulated** | Practical applications identified once basic principles are established. Formulations and preliminary architectural concepts outlined. | Conceptual architecture and functional requirements defined; assumptions cataloged. |
| **TRL 3** | **Analytical & Experimental Proof of Concept** | Active analytical modeling and preliminary benchtop/coupon laboratory testing to validate predictive physics models. | Separate element behaviors validated; numerical simulation matches analytical bounds. |
| **TRL 4** | **Component Validation in Laboratory Environment** | Basic technological components integrated to prove unified system behavior. High-fidelity digital assemblies and laboratory breadboards. | Multi-body digital integration or benchtop assembly functioning as a coherent unit. |
| **TRL 5** | **Subsystem Validation in Relevant Environment** | Fidelity of integrated prototype increases significantly. Tested in simulated operational conditions (e.g., thermal/vacuum, cryo chambers). | Subsystem prototype tested under realistic environmental loading conditions. |
| **TRL 6** | **Prototype Demonstration in Relevant Environment** | Representative engineering model or full-scale prototype demonstrated under simulated or actual operational conditions. | Engineering prototype demonstrates end-to-end functionality in a high-fidelity test setting. |
| **TRL 7** | **System Prototype Demonstration in Operational Environment** | Near-production system prototype operated in its actual intended operational environment (field deployment, orbital testbed). | Full-scale system demonstrates target operational performance in the field. |
| **TRL 8** | **Actual System Completed & Qualified** | Technology proven to work in its final flight/production form under all design envelope conditions. | Formal developmental testing, qualification audits, and safety verifications completed. |
| **TRL 9** | **Actual System Proven Through Mission Operations** | Operational deployment of the technology in its final form under actual mission and lifecycle conditions. | Routine, documented operational service and mission success criteria met. |

---

## 🎯 Application to Modular Space & Terrestrial Systems

* **TRL 1–3 (Foundations & Physics Formulation)**: Numerical simulation, parametric CAD geometry, material selection, and finite element modeling (FEM).
* **TRL 4–6 (Component Prototyping & Environmental Stress)**: Subsystem kinematic trials, sub-zero chamber tests, robotic manipulation benchmarks, and hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testing.
* **TRL 7–9 (Full-Scale Field & Operational Deployment)**: Field deployment to polar test sites, terrestrial bio-habitat construction, and spaceflight qualification.
