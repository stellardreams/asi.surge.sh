## Overview

v1.1.b extends the TRL 1 AMU concept model from v1.1.a across 24 commits on September 17–18, 2026 on `master`/`main-dev`. The work closes the caliper-towing runway (TRIAL 2/3), fixes the empty render placeholder (TRIAL 5), introduces a dense parametric rivet field for mountain-climber locomotion (TRIAL 14), and reworks the CORA-M arm from rail-mounted to rivet-climbing monkey Y with widened caliper grippers. All changes are concept sketches, not validated hardware.

## Limitations and Follow-Up

- TRIAL 7 not yet `VERIFIED`: rail removed and monkey Y added, but `F5` top+side after verification (rail touches hull, gripper within `APAS` `X=2.8`) still pending final `VERIFIED` flip.
- TRIAL 8 locomotion mechanism (hand-over-hand `CORA_PATH` `0..4` → rivet, Space ROS import) remains open.
- TRIAL 6 docking-array, TRIAL 9 structural notes, and Module II–IV (swarm, ledger, nutrient) are deferred per game-plan.
- The model is OpenSCAD CSG (`$fn=64`) with PBR colors, not Blender photorealism or validated structure.
